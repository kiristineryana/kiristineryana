### 🌷 About me

I'm a computer science student who loves turning ideas into things people can actually play with, from
browser games to automation tools. Off-screen I sculpt and paint tiny clay creations and share the
process on my channel.

> *"Make it work, then make it pretty, then make it cozy."*

| 🎮 Building | 🎨 Creating | 🌱 Learning |
|---|---|---|
| **Clay & Color**, a cozy clay sculpting game | Clay sculptures and painting videos | Full-stack apps and automation |
